100% Zero-Cloud Architecture

How Companion Pairing Works

ArmoryVault never transmits your firearms or ammunition data to any cloud servers. The Mobile Companion connects directly to your desktop over your home or local Wi-Fi router.

1
Open Mobile Pairing in Desktop Settings to display a dynamic, single-use pairing QR code.
2
Scan with the Companion App. It securely pulls your encrypted vault cache onto your phone.
3
Scan ammo boxes or log range trips anywhere (even in zero-reception outdoor ranges). Submissions wait in your desktop Sync Inbox for 1-click review.

Everything Serious Firearms Owners Need

From comprehensive C&R military surplus catalogs to custom load recipes and wear schedules, ArmoryVault is built from the ground up for enthusiasts.

AES-256 PBKDF2 Vault

Your inventory is encrypted locally using 100,000 rounds of PBKDF2 key derivation and AES-256 envelope security. Comes with emergency 64-character offline recovery keys.

Generate stark, compliant Acquisition & Disposition (A&D) ledgers formatted for 8.5x11 printing. Export full provenance dossiers with round telemetry and gunsmith histories.

Dual Wear & Time Maintenance

Track maintenance by round count OR elapsed days (e.g. 5,000 rds or 180 days). 8 tailored action-type presets for Revolvers, Pump Actions, Semi-Autos, and Vintage Bolt Actions.
