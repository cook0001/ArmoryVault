Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding security, installation, and mobile synchronization.

Yes. ArmoryVault has zero telemetry, zero analytics, and zero cloud databases. All firearm records, serial numbers, maintenance notes, and photos are encrypted directly on your local hard drive using AES-256 and PBKDF2 password derivation. No one—not even the creators of ArmoryVault—can access your records.
ArmoryVault is a free, open-source project and is not distributed with expensive corporate code-signing certificates. To open on macOS: drag ArmoryVault.app to your Applications folder, open Terminal, and run xattr -cr /Applications/ArmoryVault.app. Alternatively, visit System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
When your computer and mobile phone are connected to the same local Wi-Fi router, the Desktop application opens an encrypted local HTTP endpoint. Scanning the QR code in the desktop app pairs the companion instantly. All inventory caching, range trip logging, and batch sync occur directly peer-to-peer over your local router.
When you first configure ArmoryVault, the application generates an emergency 64-character offline recovery key. If you forget your master password, you can unlock and re-key the vault using this recovery key. Keep this key safely stored in a physical firebox or encrypted password manager.
